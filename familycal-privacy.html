familycal privacy policy

familycal is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not available to other people, and it holds no data about anyone other than the owner and the owner's family.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it can create and edit events on one Google Calendar that the app creates in the owner's own account. It may also be given read-only access to the owner's Gmail, limited to messages from a short list of school senders, to find dates and actions.

What it does with data

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, published or used for advertising. Google user data is used only to provide the calendar feature described above. If email text is ever sent to an AI service to extract dates, it is used only for that purpose.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account permissions and delete the calendar and stored files.

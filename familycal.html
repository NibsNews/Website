familycal

familycal is a private tool used only by its owner. It copies the owner's school timetable and homework into a single Google Calendar so they show on a kitchen display. It is not offered to anyone else and has no public sign-up.

It uses Google Calendar access to create and update one calendar in the owner's own Google account. Later it may also use read-only Gmail access to find dates in selected school emails.
